DJF Bangkok - UPDATES PHOTOS

Upload photos into this folder and they appear automatically in the running photo strip on the website.
The photo's FILE NAME becomes its title:

   2026-11-18_Evening-Aarti.jpg   ->  title "Evening Aarti", date 18 Nov 2026
   2026-11-20_Pooja at Mandir.jpg ->  title "Pooja at Mandir", date 20 Nov 2026

Tips
- Start the name with the date (YYYY-MM-DD) so the newest photos come first and the date is shown.
- Use jpg, png, webp or gif. Keep each photo under about 1 MB (resize big phone photos first).
- Wait 1-2 minutes after uploading, then refresh the website.
